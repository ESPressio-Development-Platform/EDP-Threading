# Build, Test and Source Map

C++20 is required. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.MD`, `docs/LIFECYCLE.MD` and `docs/RESOURCES.MD` are durable design references. Host tests, UBSan, Arduino/ESP-IDF compile-link validation and resource-measurement projects protect topology, lifecycle, cancellation, waits and deterministic RAM behaviour. `demos/basic-threading` is the primary integration demonstration.
